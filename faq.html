FAQ

Questions before we start.

A few straightforward answers about the consulting model.

Do I need a full architecture engagement?

No. We can start with one problem or one part of the application.

Can you work with an existing development team?

Yes. The service complements an existing team with senior iOS review and technical guidance.

What technologies can you review?

Swift, SwiftUI, UIKit, Objective-C, Combine, concurrency, networking and MVVM/Clean Architecture.

How does the first conversation work?

We identify the problem, choose a focused area and decide whether deeper work makes sense.
